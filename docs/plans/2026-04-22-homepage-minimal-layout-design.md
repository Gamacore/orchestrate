# Homepage Minimal Layout Design

## Goal

Reduce repetition on the homepage while preserving Arcten's core signals:

- long-horizon autonomous research and coding agents
- Y Combinator
- San Francisco
- roots in AI research at Caltech
- direct contact

## Approved Layout

1. Header row with brand on the left and `x` / `git` links on the right.
2. Short hero statement focused on what Arcten builds now.
3. Two compact proof lines:
   - `Backed by Y Combinator. San Francisco.`
   - `Roots in AI research at Caltech.`
4. ASCII art block.
5. Single contact action using the founders email.
6. Footer theme toggle remains unchanged.

## Content Rules

- Mention San Francisco once.
- Mention Caltech once.
- Remove the dedicated hiring section.
- Keep the page visually sparse and left-aligned.
- Reuse existing shared UI components where possible, especially `ChipLink`.

## Copy Direction

- Hero should be short and declarative.
- Proof text should read like quiet credentials, not marketing copy.
- Contact should be just the email link, without extra framing text.
